Inside the Centre

داخل المركز

From a warm welcome at reception to calm, spotless treatment rooms, every detail is designed for your comfort.

Reception

A welcome beneath our leaf-mandala logo

Our reception wall carries the Arabian Holistic emblem: a ring of deep green leaves around a geometric knot, set in soft beige. It reflects growth, healing and the balance we aim to bring each guest. Our uniformed team greets you here, offers a refreshment and arranges your consultation.

Ayurvedic murals

Peacocks and flowers

Vibrant hand-painted murals line our walls: blue peacocks and floral art in saffron, coral and green. They bring colour, culture and a sense of place to the quiet rooms where you rest.

Digital display at the centre

Treatment rooms

Private rooms for every therapy

Each room is prepared before you arrive: fresh linens, warmed oils and a gentle temperature.

Shirodhara room

Brass Shirodhara vessel on a stand, warm oils and a quiet, dimmed setting.

Abhyangam room

Heated oils, soft towels and a supportive treatment table for full-body massage.

Potli room

Warm herbal poultices prepared fresh and kept at a comfortable temperature.

Mukha Lepam room

Herbal pastes, brushes and gentle lighting for the Ayurvedic facial.

Akshi Tarpan room

A calm, low-light space for the eye therapy, with a reclined headrest.

Herbal preparation area

Where herbs, powders and medicated oils are measured, blended and warmed fresh for each guest.

Our staff

Our therapists wear professional dark-green uniforms with gold trim, and our practitioners wear clinical white coats. Every team member is trained to greet you warmly and to explain each step of your treatment.

Clean and hygienic

Fresh linen for every guest, sanitised equipment and tools, hand hygiene before every session, and rooms aired and reset between appointments.

Your Wellness Journey Begins Here

What your visit looks like

From the moment you walk in, you will always know what comes next.

  1. Arrive and be welcomed

    Reception greets you by name and offers a refreshment.

  2. Consult

    A short conversation about your health, sleep, stress and goals.

  3. Settle into your room

    Change in privacy while the oils are warmed and the room is dimmed.

  4. Receive your therapy

    Your therapist guides you through the treatment in unhurried silence.

  5. Rest and take guidance

    Relax with herbal tea, then take simple advice for home care.
