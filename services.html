Calm the mind

Shirodhara

الشيرودارا

Shirodhara is an ancient Ayurvedic ritual in which a thin, steady stream of warm herbal oil flows over the forehead from a hanging brass vessel. Combined with a gentle head and scalp massage, it quiets the mind and settles the nervous system.

Benefits

  • Eases stress, anxiety and mental tension
  • Supports deep, restful sleep
  • Helps relieve tension headaches
  • Supports nervous system and hormonal balance

Suitable for

Busy professionals, anyone with a restless mind or irregular sleep, and those who carry tension in the head, neck and eyes.

Warm herbal care for body comfort

Abhyangam & Potli

أبيانغام وعلاج البوتلي

Abhyangam is a rhythmic full-body massage with warm medicated oil chosen for your constitution. Potli (Pottali Swedana) follows with warm poultices filled with herbs and gently pressed over the body, carrying heat and herbal oils into tired muscles and joints.

Benefits

  • Relieves muscle stiffness and joint discomfort
  • Improves circulation and skin softness
  • Reduces fatigue and promotes restful sleep
  • Leaves the body warm, light and nourished

Suitable for

People with desk-related back and shoulder tension, active adults, those feeling run down, and anyone who wants a grounding full-body treatment.

Nature's facial for radiant skin

Mukha Lepam

Ayurvedic Facial

العناية العشبية بالبشرة

Mukha Lepam is a traditional Ayurvedic facial that uses herbal pastes, medicated oils, natural exfoliants and a therapeutic face massage to cleanse, nourish and rejuvenate the skin. Herbs are matched to your skin type.

Benefits

  • Deeply cleanses and detoxifies the skin
  • Supports an even, brighter skin tone
  • Helps soften the look of fine lines and dark spots
  • Relaxes facial muscles and eases tension

Suitable for

Anyone seeking a natural facial: dull or tired skin, uneven tone, pigmentation concerns, or a pre-event glow without harsh chemicals.

Holistic eye healing the natural way

Akshi Tarpan

Eye Therapy

أكشي تاربان

Akshi Tarpan is a soothing Ayurvedic eye treatment. A ring of dough is placed around the eyes and filled with warm, medicated ghee, which is held for a short time while you rest. It is followed by a gentle massage around the eyes and temples.

Benefits

  • Cools and relaxes tired, strained eyes
  • Eases dryness, heaviness and screen fatigue
  • Supports clarity and long-term eye comfort
  • Calms the mind through the soothing warmth

Suitable for

Heavy screen users, students, drivers, and people whose eyes feel dry, hot or tired by evening.

Clear the head, lift the senses

Nasya

ناسيا

Nasya is a classical Ayurvedic therapy for the head and neck. After a gentle facial and shoulder massage with herbal steam, a few drops of medicated oil are placed in the nostrils to clear and nourish the nasal passages.

Benefits

  • Supports clear breathing and sinus comfort
  • Eases head heaviness and tension
  • Refreshes the senses and improves focus
  • Helpful through dry, dusty or air-conditioned seasons

Suitable for

People affected by dry air, dust, seasonal sinus discomfort or frequent head tension.

Cleanse, restore, rebalance

Panchakarma Programs

برامج البانشاكارما

Panchakarma is Ayurveda's signature detox and rejuvenation approach. Your programme begins with a consultation, then combines preparatory oil therapies and herbal steam with cleansing and nourishing treatments in a sequence planned for your body type. Procedures are chosen only where they are suitable for you.

Benefits

  • Deep physical and mental reset
  • Supports digestion, energy and immunity
  • Eases chronic stiffness, stress and fatigue
  • Includes diet and lifestyle guidance to keep the results

Suitable for

Adults ready to commit time to their health, seasonal detox seekers, and those with long-standing tiredness or lifestyle-related imbalance.

Additional Ayurvedic therapies

Often added to a main treatment or taken as part of a Panchakarma programme.

Janu Basti

Warm herbal oil is held in a dough ring over the knee.

Benefits:
Knee stiffness, ache and mobility
Suitable for:
Active adults and those with knee discomfort

Greeva Basti

Warm medicated oil pooled over the neck and upper back.

Benefits:
Neck tension and stiffness from desk work
Suitable for:
Office workers and drivers

Kati Basti

Warm oil retained over the lower back inside a dough ring.

Benefits:
Lower back ache and stiffness
Suitable for:
Long hours sitting or standing

Swedanam

A gentle herbal steam bath after oil massage.

Benefits:
Opens pores, eases stiffness, supports detox
Suitable for:
Most adults, as part of a programme

Udwartanam

A dry herbal powder massage worked against the hair growth.

Benefits:
Skin tone, circulation and a lighter body feel
Suitable for:
Those seeking firming and detox support
